Asection 1. The drain (High)A

1. The drain (High)

Wallet 0x78c9b1… (the signing wallet) created the contract 0x6cebdf… in its first transaction, 0x477c8060aa22478b436d6120ffe5954775cb02974acd09d7c52ae1500b73d4d9, at 14:50:11 UTC. At 15:49:59 UTC it called that contract, which created 0xf6d072… inside the transaction and drew the five tokens out of the vesting contract. The exploit contract received 99 % or more of the gross outflow of each token, the rest went to a fee-share recipient. A block builder (Quasar Builder) took a 0.01 ETH tip in that transaction; it is a terminal.

Press describes the mechanism as an accounting flaw in the vesting manager's release function. I did not verify that independently (Medium).

Four more attempts by the signing wallet between 15:58 and 16:25 UTC reverted (nonces 2 to 5, calls into its contract).

Asection 6. Other chainsA

6. Other chains

  • The drained contract address holds no code on BSC, Base, Robinhood Chain, Optimism, Avalanche or Arbitrum. A deployment at another address is not excluded; UNCX's vesting documentation lists none and none was located.
  • Polygon, Arbitrum, Unichain, Optimism, Avalanche: no outbound token transfer after 14 Oct 15:00 on any documented contract, except Polygon legacy: 16 transfers of one token to 9 recipients, not a drain pattern.

  • Limits: token transfers only, native-coin outflows not read; the drain flag (three or more tokens to one recipient within 10 minutes) is uncalibrated.

Asection 8. Method and confidenceA

8. Method and confidence

High = read from a signed transaction, a contract creation, a Transfer log or a balance call. Medium = an inference from High facts or a press statement. Low = a hypothesis. The files: attacker_addresses.csv (4 addresses), hops.csv (57 transfers in 46 rows, identical transfers of one transaction folded into one row with a count column; transfers from the attacker addresses, the exploit contract and the funder's 22 Sep recipient, with the receiving side classified as attacker-held, terminal, victim, funder or open lead), drain_txs.csv (14 rows, 10 transactions touching the vesting contract on 13 to 15 Oct, labelled attacker, other actor, ordinary release or inbound deposit).

Terminal rule: routers, pools and a block builder end the trace. A funding wallet is listed as an attacker funder only when it both funded the attacker wallet before the drain and received funds back from it.
